## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to the Dutch parkour and freerunning community site looking for upcoming jams, where to meet and how to join. Mobile access and shareable event links are central.

### Brand Personality
Community, energetic, approachable. Use the established PKFR presentation for this change; this is the working direction pending any further preference from the creator.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing light and dark themes, typography, blue accents and community imagery. Make events easier to scan through prominent dates, clear titles and restrained practical information.

### Design Principles
- Show every upcoming agenda item in chronological order without a nested scrolling calendar.
- Give each event a shareable detail route.
- Put date, time and meeting location first, especially on mobile.
- Preserve the calendar as the content source and show missing information honestly.
- Keep keyboard navigation and both existing languages supported.
